# Product rationale

Elsewhere is a proposed nearby outdoor escape planner for people with a limited window of free time. Its central idea is to fit a complete outing into the available time: travel out, time outside, and the return journey.

Natural England's April 2024–March 2025 report found that 65% of adults in England who had not visited green space in the preceding 14 days wanted more time outdoors. The landing page states that population explicitly; it is not a global statistic or a promised health outcome. Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/the-people-and-nature-surveys-for-england-adults-data-y5q4-january-2025-march-2025/adults-year-5-annual-report-april-2024-march-2025

Existing tools validate related needs. AllTrails offers trail information and offline maps (https://www.alltrails.com/welcome), and Komoot supports duration/difficulty discovery and route planning (https://newsroom.komoot.com/226177-komoot-launches-new-discover-features-finding-personalised-tours-is-easier-than-ever/). Elsewhere's proposed focus is reducing decisions while accounting for the whole outing. It does not claim that time filters or nearby discovery are new inventions.

The proposed first release includes curated local escapes, available-time and travel-mode inputs, a return-time budget, weather/daylight notes, a packing list, and saved plans. Downloadable maps, rescheduling, shared plans, and wider city coverage would follow validation. The current website contains illustrative plans and generated landscapes, not verified destinations or navigation. Price, launch date, and geographic coverage are unannounced.
